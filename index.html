FAQ

01 Who can join BizTech?

BizTech is open to all students, regardless of major or experience level! Whether you're into tech, business, or both, we welcome anyone with a passion for learning and innovation.

02 What kinds of events does BizTech host?

We host a variety of events, including industry networking sessions with recruiters, hands-on tech workshops, and collaborative innovation challenges. There’s always something for everyone!

03 How do I join BizTech?

Joining is easy! Just fill out our quick sign-up form on the google form linked in the nav bar, and you'll be added to our member list.

04 How can BizTech help with my career?

BizTech connects you with industry leaders and recruiters, providing valuable networking opportunities. Our workshops and events are designed to enhance your resume and prepare you for future career opportunities in both business and technology sectors.

About EY

EY is our prestigious Sapphire Sponsor, supporting BizTech with their expertise in consulting, assurance, and tax services.

As a global leader, EY is committed to building a better working world by empowering businesses and fostering innovation.

Their support helps our members gain invaluable insights into professional growth, leadership, and emerging technologies.

Learn more about EY

About KPMG

KPMG, as our Sapphire Sponsor, plays a key role in offering professional services that range from audit to consulting, with a strong focus on data-driven solutions.

KPMG’s dedication to supporting young talent aligns with BizTech’s mission to bridge the gap between business and technology.

Their sponsorship opens doors for members to learn from industry experts and explore real-world challenges.

Learn more about KPMG

About Data Engine

DataEngine, our Sapphire Sponsor, is a trailblazer in data analytics and engineering solutions.

Their focus on harnessing the power of data aligns with BizTech’s vision of integrating technology with business processes.

With their sponsorship, members gain access to cutting-edge knowledge in data science and hands-on opportunities to work on impactful projects.

Learn more about Data Engine

Navigate I: KPMG Firm Visit

Our Emerald sponsor, KPMG, allowed us to host a firm visit and gave students an exclusive look at the workings of a leading professional services firm.

Attendees toured the offices and learned about various career paths directly from KPMG professionals.

The event was an excellent opportunity for students to ask questions and build connections with recruiting managers and KPMG graduates.

It offered a unique insight into the corporate culture and career opportunities at KPMG.

Navigate II: Grad Panel

The Intern and Grad Panel started our event series, offering attendees valuable insights from graduates, interns and recruiting managers at top firms like EY, KPMG.

Participants learned about the recruitment process and what it takes to stand out in today's competitive job market.

The event provided practical advice for navigating early career challenges: networking opportunities and how to do well in interviews/assessments.
